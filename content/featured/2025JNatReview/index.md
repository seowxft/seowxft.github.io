We review advances in metacognitive research and treatments across psychiatric disorders in cognitive neuroscience and clinical disciplines. We highlight their differing conceptualisations and methods, and propose a hierarchical framework that links basic neural computations of self-monitoring with higher-level clinical metacognitive beliefs to provide an integrative path forward.
