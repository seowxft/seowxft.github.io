An episode of the IOCDF Research Roundtable, a seminar series that picks a hot topic in OCD literature to dissect with expert researchers in the field. I, together with [Dr Lance Hawley](https://cognitivebehaviouraltherapy.ca/) and [Dr Jason Krompinger](https://www.newenglandocd.org/meet-jason), speak about ruimination and metacognition in OCD with host IOCDF Advocate [Kyle King](https://iocdf.org/old-advocate-program/).
